## Problem

The current Kartex logo lacks a distinctive brand identity. A more memorable logo is needed that visually communicates the app's purpose as a flashcard/learning tool.

## Solution

Design a new logo featuring a stylized "K" placed on a learning card (flashcard) motif, or alternatively a composition of multiple cards with the K integrated. Examples to explore:
- A single flashcard with a bold "K" on its face
- A fanned stack of cards with "K" prominent on the top card
- Geometric card shapes forming a "K" lettermark
