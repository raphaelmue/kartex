### Task 20-01-02 — Replace logo.svg + update AppShell brand areas

#### Step A: Replace `apps/frontend/public/logo.svg`

Write the following content exactly (no `<text>` elements, outlined `<rect>`/`<polygon>` shapes only):

```svg
<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" viewBox="0 0 512 512" width="512" height="512">
  <rect x="96" y="56" width="320" height="400" rx="32" ry="32"
        fill="#ffffff" stroke="#4f46e5" stroke-width="24"/>
  <rect x="172" y="148" width="40" height="216" fill="#4f46e5"/>
  <polygon points="212,232 212,264 336,152 336,196" fill="#4f46e5"/>
  <polygon points="212,252 212,284 336,320 336,364" fill="#4f46e5"/>
</svg>
```

**Design notes (confirm before writing):**
- Canvas: 512×512, transparent background
- Card motif: white-fill portrait rectangle, indigo (#4f46e5) border, 24px stroke-width (≥24px min for legibility at 16px favicon)
- K vertical stem: `<rect x="172" y="148" width="40" height="216">` — 40×216px filled indigo
- K upper arm: polygon quad (212,232)-(212,264)-(336,152)-(336,196) — connects stem right edge to upper-right of K bounds
- K lower arm: polygon quad (212,252)-(212,284)-(336,320)-(336,364) — connects stem right edge to lower-right of K bounds
- Arm junction overlap at stem: y=252-264 (12px) fills the V-notch of the K
- All content within 410×410 maskable safe zone (51px min padding from canvas edge; card has 96px left/right margin) ✓
- No `<text>`, no `font-family`, no external stylesheet references, no CSS classes ✓

## Key Decisions Applied

- **logo.svg shapes:** `<rect>` stem + `<polygon>` arms (not `<text>`) — RESEARCH Pitfall 1 (sharp/librsvg font availability in Docker/CI)
- **AppShell `<img>` attributes:** `alt=""` + `aria-hidden="true"` — UI-SPEC accessibility rule (WCAG 2.1 SC 1.1.1 decorative image)
- **Two brand areas:** both `<aside>` sidebar and `#mobile-nav-drawer` updated — UI-SPEC Pitfall 4 guard
- **`apple-touch-icon.png` copy:** manual `cp` after generator run — Phase 12 decision `12-01`, RESEARCH Pitfall 2
- **`@vite-pwa/assets-generator` explicit install:** NOT in any package.json yet (only a peer dep in yarn.lock) — RESEARCH Open Question 2 answer
- **Logo as `<img>` (not inline SVG or SVGR):** static brand mark, no CSS recoloring needed — RESEARCH Alternative Considered
