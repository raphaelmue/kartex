## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-24-15 | Tampering | confirmPassword sent to backend | low | mitigate | confirmPassword is client-only state; POST body is the RegisterInput `{ token, username, password }` only (Task 2, Pitfall 5) |
| T-24-16 | Spoofing | Invitee edits pre-filled email | medium | mitigate | Email rendered as a `disabled` Input and is not a RegisterSchema field; backend sets email from the invite row regardless (defense in depth) |
| T-24-17 | Information Disclosure | Error states leaking token detail | low | accept | Inline error copy is generic (D-10); reveals only used/expired/invalid, matching what the link already implies |
