<objective>
Deliver the API/backend tier for user email self-service: the shared Zod validation contract plus the three route changes that make email readable and writable.

Purpose: The backend is the single source of truth for email uniqueness and format. Every UI surface in this phase (Settings form, no-email warning, admin dialog) is a thin consumer of `GET /me` returning `email` and of the `PATCH` endpoints enforcing normalization + the `EMAIL_TAKEN` conflict contract.
Output: `UpdateEmailSchema`/`UpdateMeSchema` in the shared package (rebuilt dist), `GET /me` + `PATCH /me` extended in `auth.ts`, `PATCH /users/:id` extended in `admin.ts`, and two backend test files (one real schema-normalization test + `it.todo` route stubs per this codebase's convention).
</objective>
